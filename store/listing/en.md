## Full description
Hangyul ganada teaches you to read and write Korean, starting from the very first letter.

WRITE IT, DON'T JUST TAP IT
Every letter is traced and then written by hand. The app looks at what you drew and tells you what to fix — too small, a stroke missing, off to one side — instead of just marking it wrong.

STROKE ORDER, PROPERLY
Korean is written in a set order: top to bottom, left to right. Every letter shows you the order it is made in, numbered and animated, and after each attempt the app tells you what you did differently. It never fails you for it.

FROM ㅏ TO YOUR FIRST WORDS
Six vowels, then five consonants, then your first syllable block — you are reading Korean by the third lesson. Then 2,581 everyday words. Hear the word. Understand its meaning. See it in a simple Korean sentence. Hear the sentence. Write the Korean yourself.

BROWSE BY WHAT WORDS ARE ABOUT
Animals, Food & Drink, People & Family, Body & Health — eighteen categories, so you can learn the words you actually want next. Or just search, in Korean or in your own language.

REVIEW WHAT YOU PERSONALLY STRUGGLE WITH
Every word is remembered four ways — meaning, reading, listening, writing — and review brings back whichever one you are weakest at, tested the way that finds it. If you keep mixing up ㅓ and ㅗ, the app notices and puts the two side by side.

WHEN SOUNDS MEET
학교 is written with ㄱ and said with ㄲ. A short lesson covers the sound changes that catch every beginner, and any word with one tells you how it is really said.

LIGHT OR DARK
Follows your phone, or pick one. The writing paper stays paper either way, because that is what Korean is written on.

HEAR EVERY WORD
Every letter, word and example sentence is recorded in two Korean voices, male and female. All of it is on your device.

WORKS WITHOUT A SIGNAL
Nothing here needs the internet. No account, no sign-in, no cloud. Your practice stays on your phone.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, 한국어, 日本語, 简体中文, Español, Français, Deutsch and Português — the explanations, not just the menus.

WHAT IT IS NOT
Not a TOPIK course. Not a chatbot. Not a subscription. You buy it once and it is yours.
