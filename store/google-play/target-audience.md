# Target audience and content — the answers for Play Console

Play Console › App content › Target audience and content.

## Target age groups

**18 and over**, and only that.

The app is usable by a child and contains nothing unsuitable for one. It is not
*designed for* children, which is the question Play is actually asking:

* There is no child-directed design — no characters with names, no story, no
  rewards economy, no play mechanics.
* The instructional copy is written for an adult beginner. It assumes an adult's
  reading level and an adult's reason for being there.
* The store listing, the screenshots and the icon are aimed at adults learning a
  second language.

Declaring a younger age group would put the app under the Families policy,
which requires a designed-for-families experience the app does not attempt to
be. Declaring 18+ is the accurate answer, not the convenient one.

## Appeal to children

**No.** No cartoon protagonists, no collectible characters, no bright-primary
"kids app" styling, no voice-over persona. The one illustrated element is the
product's own mascot mark, which appears at small size in empty states and is a
brand device rather than a character with a personality.

## Store presence

**"My app is not designed for children and I do not want it in the Kids tab."**
