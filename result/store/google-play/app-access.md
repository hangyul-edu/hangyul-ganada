# App access — the answers for Play Console

Play Console › App content › App access.

## The answer

**"All functionality is available without any special access."**

That is the whole form. Every screen, every letter, every one of the words, the
review system, the settings and the sound-change lesson are reachable from a
cold install with no credential of any kind.

## Why there is nothing to give a reviewer

There is no account, no sign-in, no sign-up, no email capture, no social login,
no subscription, no in-app purchase, no server-side entitlement, no feature flag
and no promo code. There is nothing a reviewer could be locked out of, and
therefore no demo credential to supply.

A reviewer with the APK has exactly what a paying customer has.

## What a reviewer should know instead

Not access instructions — there are none — but two facts that make a five-minute
review land in the right place. Both are repeated in `review-notes.md`:

* The app works with the network off. Turning aeroplane mode on is a fair test
  and the right result is that nothing changes.
* The first screen has one primary button. Following it three times reaches a
  written Korean letter, which is the product.
