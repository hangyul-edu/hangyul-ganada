## Every string that is identical to the English one

Listed rather than counted, because a match is a candidate and not a finding, and a count would hide which. Every row below was read. They fall into five classes and none of them is source-language leakage:

1. **Nothing to translate.** `{{current, number}} / {{total, number}}`,
   `{{count, number}}`, `010`, `010-1234-5678`, `3.5`, `10,000`, `100,000,000` —
   interpolation and numerals, identical in every language by construction.
2. **A Korean word that no language translates.** `won` is the currency in Czech,
   Swedish, Turkish, Vietnamese and Filipino, and `Lv.` is the level abbreviation
   the same interfaces use.
3. **The same word.** `Problem` in German, Polish and Swedish; `Pause` in German
   and French; `verb`, `adverb` and `numeral` in Romanian, Swedish, Spanish and
   Portuguese; `interjection`, `contraction`, `Romanisation` and `min` in French;
   `letters` and `Recent` in Dutch.
4. **A loanword the language actually uses.** `OK`, `App`, `Home`, `System`,
   `Privacy`, `Item`, `Reset`, `Backup`, `Version` — Dutch, Italian, Filipino,
   Indonesian and German take these unchanged, and translating them would be
   less natural rather than more.
5. **A Latin-alphabet sort marker.** `A–Z` labels the alphabetical ordering
   control and reads the same in every Latin-script language here.
