## What this review does not establish

* **That a Korean speaker agrees.** No locale, Korean included, has been read by a
  qualified native speaker. Every rule in every gate is structural and structural is a
  proxy for a judgement.
* **That the levels mean anything to a learner.** The scale is curriculum-based and
  uncalibrated — see `docs/LEVEL_TEST_DIFFICULTY_AUDIT.md` §5.
* **That the next build looks like this one.** The bank is regenerated with a different
  distractor draw whenever the corpus moves. The rules and the reviewed pairs carry
  forward; this reading does not.
