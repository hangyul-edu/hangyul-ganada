## How the counts were made

* **Vocabulary meanings / examples corrected** — words whose meaning or example translation in that locale changed during this remediation: the copy packs and entry files diffed against commit `d06c7faa` for words that existed there, against the `worktree-changes-20260914-2355.tgz` working-tree backup for words added in this remediation, and the remediation patch files applied before that backup. The word lists are in `docs/locale-audit-v105-input.json`.
* **UI strings corrected** — interface strings whose text changed against the same commit. "Interface keys" counts every key in the locale's JSON files, plural forms separately, which is why it is larger than the 894 English strings the locale ledger counts.
* **Exercise collisions corrected** — groups of taught words that shared one gloss and were differentiated so they can be distractors for each other. Only the English gloss was rewritten (it is the gloss every other pack was translated from); in the other 31 locales a shared meaning is not corrected but *rejected*: `collideInAnyLocale` refuses the pair as a distractor at build time, and the count of such groups is shown so the size of that reliance is visible.
* **Safety findings corrected** — strings the child-safe policy or its runtime supplement refused and that were rewritten.
* **Long-text layout findings corrected** — what `qa:locales` measured on the rendered screens (overflow, sideways scroll, untranslated, empty names, overlap) at the end of the remediation; a locale with nothing measured wrong reads 0 / 0 (found / corrected).
* **Remaining human-review items** — what only a native speaker can settle: the whole word pack and interface, always, plus the quotation rows the model flagged.

## What this document does not claim

The counts say what changed and what was measured; they do not say the translations are natural. The automated translation audit finds missing, copied, placeholder and wrong-script rows and length outliers, and `docs/content-translation-review-sample.json` records what the model read; both are AUTOMATED CHECKED or MODEL-REVIEWED. The same-meaning group counts show how much the exercises rely on build-time rejection rather than on distinct translations, and a native reviewer would reduce them; that work is listed under external blockers in `docs/report.md`.
