# Support

**Hangyul ganada** · version 1.0.3

## Before you write

Most questions have an answer in the app itself, and these are the ones that
come up:

**"I have a new phone. How do I move my progress?"**
You cannot, and we would rather say so plainly than pretend otherwise. Your
record lives on the phone you learned on and there is no copy of it anywhere
else, so a new phone starts again. Earlier builds offered a file you could save
and restore by hand; it was removed because keeping a file safe for a year is
not something an app should ask of you. We would like to do this properly one
day; today it is a genuine limitation.

**"Does it work without a signal?"**
Yes, entirely. Every lesson, recording and typeface is inside the app. If
something does not work offline, that is a bug and worth reporting.

**"The app says my letter is wrong and it looks right to me."**
The app compares what you drew with the outline of that letter in the typeface
you have selected. Two things usually explain a surprise: the letter was drawn
much smaller than the box, or a stroke is missing. The feedback names which.
If you disagree with a specific judgement, report it — that is useful.

**"A word's meaning, sentence or recording looks wrong."**
Use *Report a problem* at the bottom of the word screen. It opens an email with
the word and the app version already filled in. Nothing about your learning is
attached.

**"Can I change how much help the writing guide gives me?"**
My Learning → Practice → *Practice style*. Both options show the letter; one is
fainter. There is no setting that gives you an empty box, on purpose.

**"How do I turn the daily reminder off?"**
My Learning → App → *Daily reminder*. It is off unless you turned it on.

**"How do I start again from the beginning?"**
My Learning → Reset → *Reset learning progress*. It asks once, names what will
go, and then does it. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

## Reporting a problem

The most useful report says what you did, what happened, and what you expected —
plus the app version, which is at the bottom of My Learning.

If it is about a specific word or letter, the *Report a problem* action on that
screen fills most of that in for you.

## What we cannot help with

* **Recovering a lost record.** There is no copy of it anywhere but the device
  it was made on. If the app is uninstalled, the record is gone, and nobody —
  including us — can bring it back. This is the cost of the privacy promise on
  the other page.
* **Account problems.** There are no accounts.
* **Refunds.** These are handled by Google Play and the App Store under their
  own policies, not by us.
