# Child-safe content audit

**11 September 2026 · policy 1.0.0 · audited tree: the commit that shipped
versionCode 23 (`456d4d67`) and the tree that fixed it.**
Machine record: `docs/child-safe-content-audit.json` (regenerated by
`npm run content:safety:qa`; `--check` fails on any finding or a stale file).

## 1. The defect, reproduced before anything was changed

`dict_섹스하다` was in the shipped Level Test bank
(`apps/web/public/level-test/bank-8b0e5dba.json`) four times: as a level-12
`meaning` item (prompt 섹스하다, English answer "to have sex"), as a level-12
`produce` item (answer 섹스하다 among 내리다 · 열리다 · 즐기다), and as a
distractor in `word_igida:meaning` (level 10) and `word_doragada:produce`
(level 10, options 노력하다 · 돌아가다 · 삶다 · 섹스하다). The same headword
was in the dictionary index and in `entries/s-5-….json`, glossed "to have sex",
in the web build, in both native asset copies, and in the delivered APK and
AAB under `result/`.

The gate that certified that bank — `npm run content:safety:check` — exited
**0** on the shipped tree (`scratchpad/before/content-safety-before.txt` in
the session; reproduced as `packages/content-safety/src/legacy.test.ts`).

## 2. Root cause

Three independent lists, none of which matched the string that shipped:

| Where | What it checked | Why it missed 섹스하다 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| `content/vocabulary/learner-safety.json` → `excluded` (66 terms) | whole-headword equality | it held 섹스; the headword was 섹스하다 |
| `scripts/content/build_level_test.py` → `_UNSUITABLE` (72 substrings) | substring on the headword | 섹스 was never on the list |
| `build_level_test.py` → `unsuitable()` gloss terms | substring on the English gloss | it looked for "sexual"; the gloss was "to have sex" |

Behind them, three structural faults. The dictionary — the pool the upper
Level Test levels are drawn from — was declared out of the safety layer's
scope ("a learner who searches for a word has asked for it"), so 30,334
Wiktionary headwords fed the assessment with no policy on them. The safety
gate read `produce` options and never resolved the option *ids* of `meaning`
items, so a dictionary anchor used as a distractor was invisible to it. And
the gate, the anchor builder and the bank builder each read a different list,
so no one file could be complete.

## 5. What was done

- **23 taught words retired** with tombstones (`retired-words.json`): 살인,
  강도, 무기, 총알, 미사일, 사격, 전쟁, 전투, 죽이다, 죽음, 사망하다, 숨지다,
  살신성인, 취하다, 술집, 벌거벗다, 키스, 바보, 멍청하다, 협박하다, 이념, 파업,
  좀비. Ids stay in `word-ids.json`; progress, memory, saved-word and mistakes
  rows are kept on the device; the plan repair and the review builder simply
  stop scheduling them. The 552 copy-pack rows for them were removed
  (translations remain in git history).
- **1 example rewritten** (베다: 칼에 손을 베었어요 → 풀을 베어요) with 7 inline
  and 24 pack translations and 2 new recordings; **1 Korean gloss rewritten**
  (치다: 손이나 물건으로 때리다 → 손이나 물건으로 두드리다).
- **Dictionary**: 694 headwords and 525 senses refused at publication
  (`manifest.childSafety`); 30,334 → 29,711 headwords. Death vocabulary stays
  (505 rows, CONTEXT_BLOCK, allowed in a dictionary, refused at random).
- **Level Test**: bank rebuilt — 3,995 → 3,990 items, 0 refused at
  publication once the pool was clean; retired words excluded from the
  dictionary anchor pool. **Gap-fills**: 513 → 510.
- **Audio**: manifest regenerated, 94 recordings of retired sentences removed,
  2 added.
- **Policy, evaluators, fixtures, gates, runtime**: see
  `docs/CHILD_SAFE_CONTENT_POLICY.md` §5.
